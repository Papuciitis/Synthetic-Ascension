Timeline

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset, click a spot to inspect the slowest frame there (with the chart focused: arrow keys pan, + and − zoom, 0 resets). Shaded stretches are time the recorder did not capture: it only keeps the seconds around a hitch.

Frame detail

Click a hitch marker on the timeline, or a row in the “Worst hitches” table, to see what was measured in that frame.

What the hitches were tagged as

Frames over 28 ms, counted by the tag the game gave them. A tag names the largest measured cost in the frame: a lead, not proof. Click a tag to filter the table.
