# Objective world visual QA — 18 October 2026

## Verdict and scope

**GO for the reviewed final terrain/state presentation packet. No blocking visual findings.**

All **27 final HUD-clean PNGs** were individually reopened and inspected: nine panels at 1280×720 normal, 960×540 normal, and 960×540 with Reduced Motion plus Combat Flashes Off. The final manifest records `complete=true`, 27 captures, zero capture failures and zero save writes. No engine was launched and no production files were edited for this review.

This verdict covers Wardstone, Exit Rite, District Relay, Ward Vigil and Wager presentation in the supplied current-terrain poses. It does not extend this packet to Breach, Vault/Last Chance, building/cache or Ritual Interference, which have separate capture/review work. Functional test counts are not the basis of the visual verdict.

The first capture pass was also inspected, then superseded after its cosmetic Follower notice persisted into unrelated staged panels. The final images remove that unrelated notice from sanctuary, Rite, Relay, Vigil and refusal. The `-25 Followers` notice remaining in the paid Wager views accompanies actual purchases; the refusal views show no debit notice. The first pass is preserved in `world-captures-first/` and was not hashed for this final record.

## What the final pixels establish

- **Material and contact:** broad muted stone/iron forms fit the pale paving and moss substantially better than the former ornate shrines. The obelisk stands on its base; receivers and scales have convincing low contacts; Vigil reads as an embedded flat machine; the Rite is an overhead threshold. No visible rectangular matte, distracting alpha halo, floating base or clipped objective body appears in these frames.
- **Wardstone:** temporary capture remains a small local edge around the actual player. The anchored form engages its base and gives the larger sanctuary edge priority. Its old capture ornament no longer competes with the active field.
- **Rite:** the locked cross-brace, open ready threshold, channel brackets, grace stops, outward drain marks and completed passage are different static readings. Three far-side archive plaques remain distinct from the five near-side pointed safeguard shields. Filled versus empty safeguards and durable thirds are visible at 960×540. The progress arc supports the true circle without a broad bright halo.
- **Relay:** exactly three receivers are visible; the center is a passive small summary rather than a fourth receiver. The completed shutter covers the actual painted lens, while untouched/banked sites keep an open central gap. The partial hold is a steady short bar. The completed check supplements the shutter rather than supplying the entire state.
- **Vigil:** the plate sits at the true circle center. Holding uses engaged braces and inward marks; draining drops/separates the braces and reverses the boundary directions, with explicit return text. The player remains readable in front of the low plate.
- **Wager:** the balance silhouette survives the small view. Paid tier/odds, the real next price and dwell, and “Step out to settle” are visible without colliding with the far-side tier pip. The settled fixture is aligned to the painted collection tray; its ring and paid-tier pips disappear. Its quiet status text and closed/latching tray read as inactive.
- **Refusal:** the actual no-stake refusal has a crossed next pip and an explicit reconstruction-reserve reason, without inviting “Stand to pay.” The manifest records balance 39, proposed cost 25, reserve cost 14 and charge 0; the final visual does not imply a successful payment.
- **Settings and pressure:** the important state hardware/marks remain present in all Reduced Motion/flashes-off views. In the three Rite-pressure samples, the real player/enemy silhouettes draw above the ground form and the main circle/seal information remains available around the crowd.

## Nonblocking observations

**Relay:** at 960×540 the central closed-bar versus open-gap difference is modest because the outer receiver silhouette stays the same. It is still identifiable in these frozen frames, and the banked bar plus completion check provide useful support. Keep an unfamiliar-player recognition check on the human playtest list before increasing glow, adding ornament or changing the layout.

**Wager:** the settled caption has lower contrast than the active financial instructions. That is acceptable here: the status is secondary, the tray latch is visible and no active interaction circle remains. The small secondary text is not a reason to make the spent shrine as visually loud as an available purchase.

## Evidence limits

These are controlled, frozen samples in the actual Game. The comparison panels deliberately stage multiple copies; they do not establish production spawn counts. The Rite completed flag/ledger is controlled, with no actual climax or segment handoff in that panel. Rite pressure uses 15 actual ordinary enemies from `EnemySpawner.spawn_burst_at`, not a claim that these are a naturally triggered Rite wave.

The Wager purchase and step-out settlement are real production paths according to the capture manifest; some other holds/state clocks are deliberately assigned for presentation comparison. This is source-aware pixel QA, not a blinded unfamiliar-player test. Full front/back traversal, every camera-edge approach, animated transitions, full rendered grayscale comparisons and live crowded-fight reaction time remain outside these stills. No all-run gameplay, performance or accessibility-conformance claim is made.
