## Practical limits

- These are frozen controlled transitions with an immortal player. Motion, decision speed during a crowded fight, comfort and full-run balance still require playtesting.
- The manifest identifies **two genuine tagged cache pickups**, but they overlap into one compact visual cluster. The pixels establish visible reward presence, not two separately countable item silhouettes.
- The building's five real enemies also overlap in this controlled composition. The provided stills do not establish spacing/pathing quality or every generated doorway.
- Ordinary-vault text communicates Hunter/wedge costs; these images do not prove both encounter placements succeeded. The manifest explicitly preserves their conditional production placement limit.
- The Last Chance view contains actual Rite pulse feedback around the threshold. The all-safeguards bill, open vault and 3→0 shield ledger carry the sacrifice meaning; the pulse alone should not be read as the cost indicator.
- The supplied healing-lock checks deliberately reset the independent cost control before each genuine open. No natural 45-second expiry or elapsed-time claim is inferred.
- Hazard return is inspected midway through its queued delay, not through a full revival/expiry sequence. Related authored Segment 1 rooms and other procedural seeds are outside this 24-image visual approval.
