# Objective family — independent designer final review, 18 October 2026

## Decision

**GO for the revised family in the static current-Game states shown.** No blocking material, contact, centering, physical-state or sampled actor-overlap defect was found in the final 69-image packet. The v1 jewelry-like shrines and misleading decorative duplicate sites have given way to coherent practical forms with useful physical state differences.

**Retain a separate small-window and moving-combat acceptance gate.** Fine Wager costs/settlement copy, Rite secondary text/counts and Relay's mechanical shutter/banked-progress cues still need focused recognition checks at 960×540. This is not comprehensive human-readability approval, a completed playtest, proof of encounter pacing, or approval of every future environment.

This review judges the implemented weathered-city objective scope against its actual current terrain/player. The human's evolving Mediterranean / medieval / industrial city concept is a separate whole-environment direction; its absence is not a reason to reject these objectives or regenerate them again.

## Method and evidence

Three independent designer subreviews divided the final-ready packet into world 27, risk 24 and Breach 18. Each image was individually opened with `view_image` after the final-ready notification. The lead designer consolidated those pixel findings, read their complete notes and the final manifests, checked recorded native exits, and recorded a 69-file SHA-256 snapshot. This review does not borrow the implementation chat's separate reviewer verdicts. Earlier packets with stale cosmetic toasts and historical prototype images are not the reviewed final evidence.

## Concrete follow-ups

1. **P2 — simplify and enlarge the important small-window instructions.** Wager's next price and “Step out to settle,” and Rite's secondary state/count explanations, are very fine at 960. Prioritize decision-critical information over supplementary wording; avoid solving this with brighter rings or additional glow.
2. **P2 — keep Relay's banked state legible in motion.** Its physical shutter and stored partial bar are visible in side-by-side stills, but completion checks are the strongest immediate signal. If recognition fails while moving, broaden the aperture/shutter or persistent partial bar rather than introduce color-only state differences.
3. **P2 — correct reward wording.** All three `building-reward-*` cards say “optional reward recovered” while the item remains on the floor. The local “Security cleared · loot on floor” cue is truthful. Prefer “reward available” / “loot revealed” until collection. This is copy polish rather than a blocker for the physical treatment.
4. **Evidence cleanup — Wager normal screenshot.** `wager-normal.png` has a Ward Vigil summary in the top-right over Wager samples; its small/reduced counterparts use the Wager summary. Document or refresh that staging mismatch before using the normal frame as an explanatory publication image. This does not establish a production routing bug. The genuine current-panel Wager transaction toast agrees with the manifest; do not confuse it with the superseded carried-over notices.

No further bulk prop regeneration is justified by this packet. Human checks should determine whether these focused refinements are necessary before a broad readability sign-off.

## Integration judgment

Broad muted stone, dark hardware and sparse brass belong to the sampled weathered paving/moss and compact outlined actors. No obvious hovering base, hard rectangular matte, perched low-object footprint or jewelry-like value mismatch was found. Exact boundaries are quieter than active progress and body silhouettes. Physical apertures, tray/shutter states, shield fill, broken/solid arcs and persistent words provide meaning beyond hue or animation.

Player-in-front/behind and source-adjacent enemy examples are bounded overlap evidence. They do not replace all-side traversal, high-density projectile/hazard layering, moving roof fades or a full objective completion/transition. No grayscale or vision-deficiency simulation was supplied. The human's newer city art direction also needs a later integration check if its terrain/lighting materially changes.
