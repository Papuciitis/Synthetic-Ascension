# Segment 5 native sprite previews

All four clips are 14 gameplay seconds in the actual production Game/court/player/HUD at normal camera zoom. They use native player input and actual boss AI/physics. The 1280x720 and 960x540 windows retain the 1920x1080 canvas.

**Every complete clip uses visibly captioned immortal capture mode.** These are motion/readability samples; they establish no avoidability or balance claim. Actual-damage walking, dash, recovery melee, Runner and ambient kill-trigger controls are recorded separately in the [audit](../../../audits/2026-10-16-s5-teaching-fights.md). The first real-damage Tank capture died before export and is retained as an aborted diagnostic.

Tank starts at a disclosed49% of normal maximum HP to trigger its one real Runner wave. Mage starts at full HP. Ambient spawning is disabled only in these isolated captures. The clips are silent; they do not approve the SFX mix.

Sampling requests8frames/s. Encoding preserves the measured wall intervals and final hold, without resizing, repainting or interpolating. This sampled playback is not a performance benchmark. Each encoding sidecar retains original frame hashes and timing. Selected PNGs are byte-identical source frames.
