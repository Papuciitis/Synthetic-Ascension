# Complete district concept set

2026-10-19. Human request: concept art for every listed segment/theme, a parallel common-building rollout, and a separate chat developing distinctive generation and building types. These are visual development targets, not screenshots or claims that the depicted features already exist.

## Shared brief

Use the accepted `../2026-10-18-city-directions/08-mediterranean-medieval-industrial-city.png` as the common material, camera and city-language reference. Inspect it before generating variants. Current native reference: `../../reference/2026-10-18-city-block/ground-review-11/street-ground-transition-960x540.png`.

Bright, temperate Greece/Rome/Mediterranean civic masonry layered with medieval European inhabited buildings, later modest industrial/steampunk services and restrained authoritarian early sci-fi details. Cool limestone and worn plaster; slate and muted terracotta; aged iron/copper; occasional dull brass. Broad readable forms, plausible roofs/chimneys, continuous walls and purposeful door openings. Natural bent streets and irregular plots. Soft broad material variation, contact/eave shading and directional sunlight; carefully placed warm practical lamps.

Each image is one wide landscape gameplay-shaped environment concept. North-up orthographic/top-down game view with pitched roofs and visible south-facing facades, no horizon, no rotated isometric diamond map. Keep the established human/door scale. Depict several coherent buildings and a generous connected playable ground area. At most three tiny people as scale reference. Ground and roofs need quiet broad planes; detail clusters belong to frontage, roof fixtures, structural joints and landmarks. Buildings should have volume and believable joins, not flat roof boxes or black outlines on every tile. No HUD, UI, captions, signatures or legible text inside images.

Avoid beige desert cities, night/noir drift, excessive bloom, neon circuits across paving, random pipes/chimneys, uniformly gridded blocks, equal-strength micro-outlines, wall holes, decorative obstacles across required doors and streets. All themes belong to the same city, but need distinct architectural silhouettes, useful landmarks, prop groups and route shapes, not only recolouring.

Generate each listed image independently with the built-in image tool. Reference painting supplies style/camera/materials, not the copied street layout. Preserve original generated output; save each final PNG in this folder. Record exact prompt, tool mode and source/output paths and SHA256. No bitmap editing/compositing through scripts.
