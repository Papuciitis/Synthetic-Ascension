# Donjon dungeon concept direction

2026-10-19. The human requested recognizable dungeon concepts after reviewing the open Donjon regions and large roofless facilities.

These two proposals carry the accepted city materials into fortified dungeon architecture. They are generated concept paintings for review; the current game has not acquired these buildings.

## Exterior

[01 Fortified Donjon exterior](01-fortified-donjon-exterior.png) establishes a substantial gatehouse, enclosing masonry, slate-roofed wings, a keep and an arcaded courtyard. Secondary clay roofing and limited copper service equipment connect it to the inhabited city. Vegetation belongs to planted edges and courtyards. Broad daylight and portal shadows preserve the pale and dark contrast.

The useful production elements are a gate module, repeated wing bays, roof junctions, a keep or tower accent and one attached industrial service group. Derive their dimensions and positions from the actual site. Preserve real entrances and accessible routes while selecting modules deterministically.

## Interior

[02 Donjon interior cutaway](02-donjon-interior-cutaway.png) shows a connected main hall with clear side rooms, cells, storage and a mechanical service chamber. Strong wall thickness, framed passages, iron grilles, columns and limited amber or teal light establish the dungeon identity. Quiet midtone floors leave space for actors and combat tells.

The room functions are visual examples, not new story canon or reward rules. Cutaway art should reveal actual walkable interiors, with roof and frontage ownership following the real building. Keep the floor, wall collision and doorway representation in agreement.

## Implementation boundaries

The paintings reorganize the supplied floor plan, including its entrance composition. They are not exact topology proposals. An implementation must use actual SitePlan walls, doors, keepouts and streaming ownership; it must not place a copied painting over the existing maze.

The facilities need a dedicated architectural family. The current CA Donjon example reads as open lanes and paving islands, so it cannot demonstrate this enclosed-dungeon target. Its indoor or outdoor contract must be resolved deliberately before applying architecture.

Retain the continuous-envelope and facility seam repairs. Avoid redundant inner wall rings in small interiors. Preserve meaningful partitions in larger successful room layouts. Apply the same complete-wall and route checks to any future dungeon family.

For runtime art, simplify the concepts further: broad roof planes, selective masonry edges, grouped foliage and restrained lights. The illustrations still contain more repeated microdetail than the desired final game treatment.
