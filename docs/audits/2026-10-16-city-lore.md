# First-city optional source documents — 16 October 2026

## Implemented scope

Eight approved source documents supplement the existing nineteen Chronicle records. The implemented collection route is **Papers**, a sixth managed hub station beside the Quiet Alcove's existing **Browse** entry. Opening the tray lists available documents; inspecting a title shows its source and approved field excerpt. **Keep a copy** records the fuller Chronicle, while **Leave** returns without collecting it. Copied documents remain identifiable and grant no second pickup reward.

Collection uses `Global.grimoire_note("record:<id>")` and the existing profile save operation before returning control. The existing Library supplies Chronicles, sealed clues, full-text search, unread state and bookmarks. There is no separate lore inventory, currency, death reward or Follower requirement. The full text waits in Chronicles rather than opening over play.

The authored IDs, titles, sources, excerpts and full bodies are in `data/narrative/CityLoreDocuments.gd`. A read-back comparison against `docs/design/2026-10-16-first-city-story-proposal.md` confirmed all eight titles, excerpts and bodies match exactly. The original nineteen records in `StoryLines.RECORDS` remain untouched. Availability and guidance metadata may be corrected without changing the approved document prose.

## Availability is separate from discovery

`StoryDirector.note_progress(segment)` records played-segment arrivals and observed opening milestones in existing profile flags. A hub call passes no played segment: its counter names the next district and cannot prove that district was entered. Progress flags make copies available; they never collect or mark them read.

The recovery tray accepts durable completed best progress, completed Method/Doctrine/Apotheosis commitments and Area I completion. A Method commitment proves completed S3, Doctrine completed S6, and Apotheosis completed S9. None proves entry into the following segment. Successful S10 completion makes all eight copies available, including at the next accessible hub or Library route, without requiring death, another boss or a failed escape.

## Five physical placements deferred

The approved proposal permits using the safe hub source when a field location cannot guarantee safe inspection. This first implementation does not add field pickups or pause an active horde. Environmental discovery is deferred at exactly these five proposed placements:

1. Admission desk: the clipped service-lamp maintenance slip after admission.
2. Segment 2: the service-courtyard notice board's Containment extract.
3. Segment 4: the washhouse shutter in Civilian Cut-through or displaced laundry basket on Collapsed Ward's safe approach.
4. Segment 6: the inside of a workshop door in Industrial Cut-through or reused service door in Ruined Service District.
5. Segment 10: the gatehouse paper shelf on the safe arrival side before the final fight.

The interval annotation, Bren cover sheet and reconstruction preparation sheet already belong at the hub. All eight texts remain optionally collectible through the implemented tray across route variants and later attempts. The tradeoff is loss of their proposed environmental presentation in this pass, not loss of their text.
