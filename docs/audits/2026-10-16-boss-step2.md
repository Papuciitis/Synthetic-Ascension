# Boss overhaul step 2 and visual follow-up

2026-10-16. Implements the user's approved second delivery step from the boss proposal, together with beam, Pulse/Bomber, attack-identity and windup feedback. The random Bulldozer/Arcanist choice stays. Later moveset, phase, finished animation and reward redesign are outside this step.

## First checkpoint: usable procedural courts

Both boss roles previously had semantic plazas without guaranteed fighting space: plaza cells mainly excluded decoration, while walls/half-cover and generic center landmarks could still intrude. Independently jittered arena roots also disagreed with the plaza center.

`BossArenaSpace` now defines a fixed chunk-center anchor and reserved S5 20×20-cell (1280 px) / S10 28×28-cell (1792 px) court. District generation protects the court before blockers and dressing are placed, paves the same region and connects it to the existing road reservations. Generic center landmarks are suppressed for these roles. The encounter builder uses the same deterministic center. No sealed doors or new mandatory encounter objectives are added.

Verification: the production-generator regression initially failed **257 passed / 471 failed**. The completed test passes **728/0**, covering 88 generated arena chunks across 40 spread seeds, repeat determinism, full court reservation, blockers, body-safe socket paths, perimeter, 16-direction rush/shock/beam lanes and dressing. The existing procedural reachability suite passes **3840 chunks, 2538 doors, 1728 rewards, zero failures**. Independent review found no additional spatial defect. [Evidence](evidence/2026-10-16-boss-step2/).

This guarantees the reserved court and connected access, not every point in the surrounding district. Current leash values still permit movement beyond the court; later committed attack-placement work must respect actual surrounding geometry. The player's camera follows the player, so an entire 680 px beam is not guaranteed to be visible from every position/orientation.

## Four boss forms at gameplay scale

Each boss now uses a dedicated S5/S10 transparent raster form at unit root scale. The 77-world-pixel occupied human reference is compared with all four player races, ordinary Brute/Herald and four bosses on a shared baseline. Bulldozer anatomy is 88 px in both tiers; Arcanist is 82/86 px. Armor, staff and casting apparatus carry the tier difference. Source-space anatomy excludes equipment, and both measured envelopes are recorded separately.

The existing walking circles and square contact shapes are preserved explicitly, with private shape resources. S5 Bulldozer/Arcanist radii remain 39.015/36.72; S10 remains 44.795/42.16. EnemyWorld updates the same registered handle and invalidates its maximum-radius cache when needed. Presentation follows the player's existing mid-body combat origin, with feet 42 px below it; an independent review caught and corrected an initial feet-at-origin mistake before delivery. Ordinary enemy setup is unchanged.

The four forms are rough single-pose studies, including braced/casting poses, installed for in-game review. They are not finished walking/attack animation or human-approved final art. In particular, the Arcanist's preserved contact square is wider than its robe; touch feel still needs a human playtest before deciding whether to change established geometry. ## Raster warning, windup and impact identity

The beam's opaque dark backing is replaced with transparent irregular energy strands. Slam now uses stone fractures and rubble; shock uses branching gold crackles; mines retain their own violet sigil through the fuse and release. A shared finely engraved boundary marks the actual danger radius, while the interior art distinguishes the attack. Slam debris dissipates cosmetically after impact; terrain collision and navigation are unchanged.

Bulldozer has a full committed charge-arrow lane and a bracing accent. Arcanist has visible casting accents during fan, mine and beam anticipation. These use actual Sprite2D raster art, with existing attack clocks, fixed geometry and source cancellation. The previous fan-cone asset remains, alongside the new casting accent; this step does not claim every legacy enemy effect has been replaced.

Pulse uses a finer two-layer force ripple while retaining pooling, duration and expansion. Bomber uses a fixed-radius explosive perimeter, building fuse embers and a finite sprite blast; its 105 px payload and 0.35 s fuse are unchanged. Essential bounds persist when decorative flashes are disabled, and focused checks cover pause, cancellation and live accessibility settings.

## Sound replacements after listening feedback

The first Sci-fi Sounds selection is no longer registered for these attacks. Rejected metal/mining pings are also excluded. Recorded material now supplies a weighted brace for Bulldozer anticipation, a dedicated thud for slam/shock/charge-end impacts, an air swish for fan/pylon cues, electrical crackle for beam release and a compact 0.66 s mine blast. The accepted mine source body is retained with a shorter controlled tail. Attack timing is unchanged.

[Current effect-to-file mapping and playable clips](../../assets/audio/sfx/bosses/CREDITS.md) include durations, gains and exact triggers. Source licenses, hashes and editing recipes are retained; beam audio requires the included CC BY 3.0 attribution. These choices incorporate the user's feedback but are not claimed to have been aesthetically auditioned by the assistant. Final in-game loudness and weight need a human listen.

Independent review caught that non-resource credits were missing from the export filters. All current presets now include the root notice and audio credits/licenses. The existing release packager copies a readable `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` beside the EXE/PCK before calculating its integrity manifest. A regression exercises the real packager with inert export files: valid **9/10 RED**, then **21/0 GREEN**. This verifies distribution wiring without claiming a full game release export.

Keeping the random S10 choice does not make those later systems complete.
