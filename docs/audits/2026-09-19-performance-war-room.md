# Performance war room, 2026-09-19

What the enemy simulation costs today at 60 / 100 / 180 / 300 live
enemies, what the last month of captures says about real-play lag, what is
already built, and a prioritized plan with a benchmark gate per milestone.
Measurements here are headless CPU truth on this machine (i7-8650U, Godot
4.7.2); rendering is measured separately and only on a display.

Sources: `tools/tests/EnemyHordeBenchmark.tscn` (fresh runs, results in
`performance_results/benchmarks/`), `tools/tests/ChunkColdStreamProbe.tscn`
(new), `tools/tests/ChunkStreamingPerformanceAudit.tscn`,
`docs/diagnostics/2026-09-06-sustained-combat-profile.md`,
`docs/audits/2026-09-15-performance-captures.md`,
`docs/PERFORMANCE_PATCH_CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/OPTIMIZATION_HANDOFF.md`, the
build simulator's frame columns, and the code under
`core/systems/enemy_world/`, `autoload/EnemySimulationScheduler.gd`,
`core/combat/projectile/`, `core/systems/world/ChunkManager.gd`.

## 1. What is already built (do not redesign this)

| Concern | State | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Node-based versus data-oriented enemies | Logical population lives in `EnemyWorld` data records; only the nearest `materialized_budget` 64 (hard ceiling 96) are `CharacterBody2D` actors, activated within 480 px and released past 640 px, at most 4 promotions and demotions per step. Everything else is a data-only proxy. | `EnemyRepresentationPolicy` |
| Proxy simulation | 10 Hz, sliced (6 slices normal, 8 under pressure, 12 in emergency), steered by the threaded flow field; hit through data-side radius and segment queries; proxy deaths pay Followers and Luck like actors and emit `enemy_defeated`. | `EnemyProxySimulation`, `EnemyCombatService` |
| Proxy rendering | MultiMesh renderer for proxies; MultiMesh blockers per chunk; impact bursts batched. | `EnemyProxyRenderer`, `ChunkBlockRenderer`, `ImpactBurstRenderer` |
| Update-frequency tiers and AI LOD | Actors sit in full / mid / far tiers (budgets 32 / 32, mid in 3 groups, far in 7), spatial bands (full to 1200 px, mid to 1800 px, hysteresis), adaptive budgets under physics pressure (8 ms) and budget pressure (14 ms), an emergency tier at 20 ms (8 / 16) and a severe fast path at 40 ms, smart-physics release distances per pressure state. | `EnemySimulationScheduler` |
| Spatial partitioning | `EnemyIndex` spatial hash (64 px cells) with population caps by special kind (summoned 36, split 48, boss adds 24, beats 24, specials 72). | `autoload/EnemyIndex.gd` |
| Stale cleanup | Spawner culls at 90% of cap (8 per tick, 0.75 s), pools ambient scenes (32 per scene, warm 6), elites protected from culling and demotion. | `spawner.gd`, `EnemySimulationScheduler._is_protected` |
| Projectiles | Ordinary ballistic projectiles run in dense arrays (capacity 4096) in `_process` (moved off the physics step in August); exotic homing / beam / mine attacks stay node-owned by design. Barrage fragments retarget through a data-side lowest-health scan, at most 48 reacquisitions per frame (September 16). | `ProjectileSimulationManager`, `EnemyWorld.lowest_health_in_radius` |
| Tree attack budget | Slash and impact attacks generated by the Ascension tree are queued and flushed 12 per frame (September 15 fix of the chain-burst lag). | `AscensionRunner.flush_attacks` |
| Chunk streaming | Desired chunks planned per re-centre, activated from a queue under a 2 ms budget and a per-frame cap; blockers batched; build phases timed per chunk (setup, ground, content, floor, blocker), exposed to the overlay and, since today, the newest chunk's phase split to every flight-recorder sample. | `ChunkManager` |
| Instrumentation | Flight recorder samples carry wall-clock spacing, the tree's per-frame costs, projectile time and chunk stream stats; incidents at 28 ms frames; `tools/perf/analyze_captures.py`; deterministic headless benchmarks for hordes, pressure, projectiles, Barrage chains, chain bursts, the recorder itself. | `PerformanceFlightRecorder`, `tools/tests/*Benchmark*` |

## 2. Measurements

### 2.1 Headless horde at the requested populations (today's code)

`EnemyHordeBenchmark`, one stage per run, 20 s hold after a 4 s spin-up,
segment 2 world, player stationary, ranged style, god mode:

| Live enemies | Frame avg / p95 / p99 (ms) | Process p95 | Materialized (actors) | Data-only | Tier full / mid | Physics on |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 60 | 6.97 / 7.14 / 8.33 | 7.56 | 60 | 0 | 32 / 28 | 60 |
| 100 | 6.96 / 6.94 / 6.94 | 11.75 | 64 | 36 | 32 / 32 | 64 |
| 180 | 6.94 / 7.14 / 8.33 | 10.17 | 64 | 116 | 32 / 32 | 64 |
| 300 | 7.04 / 7.22 / 9.98 | 12.74 | 64 | 236 | 32 / 32 | 64 |

The August 27 runs of the same benchmark: 120 -> 8.3 ms p95, 250 -> 16.5,
400 -> 14.1, 550 -> 27.2 (physics p95 38). Reading: past 64 live actors
the cost curve is flat because the population above the budget is
data-only; the 180 barrier the project started from no longer exists in
the simulation. The `physics p95` column is Godot's windowed monitor and
includes render sync; it is not a per-frame physics cost (recorder note,
September 16).

### 2.2 What real play measured (captures, rendered)

- September 6 profile: frame p95 rises steeply after about 80
  *materialized* enemies; process side dominant in 97 of 106 incidents;
  projectile and full/mid-tier work scale with the same population; flow
  rebuilds and the recorder's own snapshots are not the sustained cause.
- September 14: the fight lag was the tree's kill chains spawning slash and
  impact nodes per kill (physics 337 ms, 12k draw calls); fixed by the
  attack budget (September 15).
- September 15: Barrage fragment reacquisition (51 -> 3.3 ms per update
  at 120 forced retargets after the fix), recorder deltas that were not
  wall-clock (fixed), and chunk activations of 30-77 ms mid-run with 1-7
  cached enemies (open).
- Build simulator (this week, headless): the worst per-build p99 frames
  are set VFX churn (Lattice's Index Commit spawns up to 19 VFX nodes per
  triangle, Mass Arrest's magic path ten impacts per slam), 16-30 ms at
  p99 and up to 75 ms max; Lattice builds run a 4.5 ms p95 median against
  1.4 ms for the other sets.

### 2.3 Chunk streaming, cold (new probe, today)

`ChunkColdStreamProbe`: a fresh seed, the centre walking one chunk east
per step, every queued chunk activated each step, per-phase costs
collected.

| Seed / steps | Activations | Total p50 / p95 / max (ms) | Content share | Over 2 ms | Over 16.7 ms | Worst chunks |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 902611 / 10 | 75 | 0.40 / 2.69 / 43.96 | 73% | 9 | 2 | (0,-2) initial 43.96 (content 42.6); (-2,-1) initial 23.4 (content 22.3) |
| 123457 / 20 | 125 | 0.41 / 1.98 / 40.14 | 65% | 6 | 2 | (0,-1) initial 40.1 (content 39.1); (5,0) streamed 23.1 (content 22.1) |

Three facts. Setup, ground, floor and blocker phases never exceed 3 ms.
The first activations of a fresh seed pay 40 ms once (cold resource
loads; the warmed audit pre-builds exactly that chunk and then asserts
median < 4 ms, max < 12 ms, which still holds). And one streamed chunk in
125 costs 22-23 ms entirely in content generation: the site chunks
(`SiteOverlayImpl.decorate_chunk` with its indoor volumes and door
thresholds). That is the mid-run hitch the September 15 captures saw,
and the only phase worth staging.

## 3. What the numbers do and do not say

- The simulation side is solved for the counts you asked about: 300 live
  enemies cost the same frame as 60 because 236 of them are data. The
  remaining CPU risks are the *materialized* 64 under combat load (2.2),
  set VFX churn, and site-chunk activation.
- Rendering is unmeasured in every number above. The August desktop runs
  put render CPU at about 1.5 ms and GPU about 5 ms at 550; nothing since
  has re-measured it after the V4 tree, the sets' VFX and the proxy
  renderer changes. Every "playable" claim needs a rendered capture.
- Real play has three other lag sources the benchmarks do not exercise:
  the player moves (streaming, flow field re-centre), builds fire dozens of
  payload kinds per frame, and enemies die in bursts (drops, VFX, kill
  chains). The captures, not the benchmarks, are the judge.

## 4. Prioritized plan

Ordered by expected frame-time return per unit of risk. Each milestone
names its gate; a milestone ships only with its gate green and the
previous milestones unchanged.

| # | Milestone | Design | Gate (benchmark) | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 | **Rendered baseline** (needs the display) | Run `EnemyHordeBenchmark` windowed at 60 / 180 / 300 and a stationary Endless Lunge + Mass Grave capture at seg6 with the recorder's extended features; record render CPU / GPU from `viewport_get_measured_render_time_*`. | A filed table in this document; no code. | none |
| M1 | **Site chunk staging** | Split `SiteOverlayImpl.decorate_chunk` into plan (pure data, deterministic from the seed: parcels, walls, door cells) and instantiate (nodes). Plan the next ring's site chunks one frame ahead in `queue_missing_chunks` (or on `WorkerThreadPool` if the plan is node-free), and instantiate indoor volumes across two activations. The blocker phase stays synchronous so collisions never lag the visual. | `ChunkColdStreamProbe`: no streamed activation above 12 ms over 20 steps on three seeds; `ChunkStreamingPerformanceAudit` unchanged; `ChunkStreamingSchedulerTest`, `ChunkStreamReplanThrottleTest`, `ChunkBlockIntegrationTest` green. | medium: a half-built site visible for a frame; navigation revision must commit after the last phase (`commit_pending_nav_revision` already defers to the queue's end). |
| M2 | **Set VFX pooling** | One pooled scene per VFX kind (pulse ring, spokes, arc line, cleave arc, shockwave) with a cap on simultaneous instances per kind; Lattice's triangle and Mass Arrest's slam draw from the pool. | Build simulator: Lattice melee p95 median from 4.5 ms to under 2 ms, Mass Grave p99 under 16 ms; `SetRunnerTest`, `SetScalingV2Test` unchanged. | low: visual only. |
| M3 | **Hitch tagging** | Tag every recorder sample over 28 ms with the dominant subsystem from the fields already carried (tree tick / flush / hits, fragments, projectile ms, chunk phase, flow publish, proxy slice) and print the tag distribution in `analyze_captures.py`. | Next playtest's incidents carry a tag; the distribution names the top cause without a manual read. | none |
| M4 | **Materialized budget decision** (yours) | Either raise `materialized_budget` 64 -> 96 (the ceiling) so proxies near the player deal contact damage sooner, or give proxies within 200 px a data-side contact tick. Both are gameplay decisions; the August handoff left them open. | Horde 180 / 300 p95 within 2 ms of today at 96; playtest feel. | design |
| M5 | **Scheduler refresh cost** | Profile `EnemySimulationScheduler` assignment (0.2 s interval, incumbent bias 0.9) at 300-550 live; move the ranking to a data-side pass over `EnemyWorld` arrays if it shows above 1 ms per refresh. | Horde 550 p95 from 27 ms toward 20 ms; `EnemyPressureBenchmark` gate (20% over its baseline arm) kept. | medium |
| M6 | **Rapier 2D A/B** (your go-ahead) | The biggest remaining physics lever for the 64 actors (August: 30 ms p95 at about 90 bodies); third-party binary, may change `move_and_slide` feel. | Horde 300 physics monitor and the pressure benchmark's physics step, both arms. | high: feel |
| M7 | **GDExtension hot loops** | Only if M5 and M6 leave the proxy slice or the projectile `_simulate_one` above budget at 550+: proxy step, batch fill, projectile sim. | Minigun benchmark massacre stage p95 from 39 ms toward 25 ms. | high: build pipeline |

Not on the list because they are done: attack budget (September 15),
fragment targeting and recorder trust (September 16), projectile
simulation in `_process`, impact VFX batching, MultiMesh proxies and
blockers, population caps by special kind (the summoner snowball of the
August handoff is closed by `summoned_population_cap` 36).

## 5. Verification protocol for every milestone

1. Headless: the milestone's gate benchmark, three runs, medians reported.
2. Regression: the suites named in the gate, plus `ScriptParseAuditTest`.
3. Rendered (display): a two-minute seg6 capture with the recorder's
   extended features on the same route and seed as M0, incidents tagged
   (M3), compared with the M0 table.
4. Filed under `performance_results/benchmarks/` and one dated paragraph
   in `docs/PERFORMANCE_PATCH_CHANGELOG.md`.

## 6. What was changed today

- `ChunkColdStreamProbe.tscn`: the cold, walking, per-phase streaming
  profile (section 2.3), read-only.
- The flight recorder's `chunk_stream` block carries the newest chunk's
  phase split, so an incident during a site activation names the phase.
  `FlightRecorderSampleTest` and `PerformanceFlightRecorderTest` pass.
- Fresh horde numbers at 60 / 100 / 180 / 300 (section 2.1).

Nothing in the plan is implemented beyond the instrumentation.
