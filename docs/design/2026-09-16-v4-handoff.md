stability passes, regression coverage and playable presets, preserving the
combat feel. This note is the handoff: scope delivered, how it was
verified, what the numbers are, what is approximated, and what is still
owed. The staged plan and every interpretation taken along the way are in
`2026-09-16-v4-full-implementation-plan.md`; per-node status is
`v4_implementation_matrix.md` (regenerate with
`python3 tools/design/build_v4_matrix.py`).

## 2. Verified, implemented-unverified, awaiting playtest

**Verified by tests (headless, real enemies, real projectiles, the
player's damage path):** every rule the matrix marks implemented has at
least one check in its discipline suite or the Fusion suite; the shared
rules in SharedRules/Hybrid/Ledger; every preset's purchase order, prices,
equipment, engine build and scripted fight; the whole-system behaviours
(despawn mid-effect, handle reuse, reentrant kills, pause/resume, refunds
with live state, save/load, scene transition, Revelations toggle).

**Implemented, not verified by a test (recorded partial):**

- Throw (DO10) needs terrain, which the headless world lacks.
- One Bullet's extra-projectile bonus is not applied to Barrage side rounds.
- Carry (MOQ5) drags one normal; friendly Mines/Sigils/Wells are not carried.
- Emergency Stop, Bullet Hell, Falling Gavel, Gather and Counterfeit keep
  the approximations noted in the matrix from stage 3a.
- Control conversions: every "stagger" is a stun; there is no stagger bar,
  control caps or immunity.
- HUD feedback: every Q slot names its resource and every V slot its state;
  payloads and placed objects are runner-drawn shapes, lines and text.

**Recorded n/a (no replacement mechanic invented):** Immovable (nothing
displaces the player), Aftershock's reverse knockback, Wild Card (moot),
Fortune (out of combat scope), plus the two earlier ones in the matrix.

**Awaiting a rendered playtest (checklist §3):** whether chains read as
chains, placeholder feedback legibility, the feel of held Qs (Guard,
Deadshot, Designate, Repulse), world-time slows, Throw against real
terrain, and the rendered frame spikes listed below.

## 3. Numbers

Full tables with machine, build, seed and loadout:
`docs/audits/2026-09-16-v4-build-checks.md`.

- Headless preset fights (60 bodies, 240 frames): frame p50 6.9 ms for
  every preset, p99 at or under 27 ms; the heaviest scripted fight is
  Dominion pure (p99 26.7 ms, 1,537 generated attacks in 4 s).
- Real game, headless, 40 s at population 60: p99 at or under 16.6 ms for
  all ten builds; the tree's own p95 is 0.03-5.1 ms per frame.
- Real game, rendered (Intel UHD 620, OpenGL, vsync): Execution pure p95
  25 ms, Dominion pure p95 16.7 ms, the Three-Core avalanche p95 34.7 ms
  with a 717 draw-call median.
- Single worst frames of 22-91 ms exist in every long run; they coincide
  with the probe's 60-body spawn bursts and with a few single-frame tree
  spikes (Dominion's forced movement of a full Well or a KNEEL slam; a
  DECIMATION or Gavel chain resolving many kills in one flush). These are
  the next performance targets and were not smoothed by cutting damage,
  interactions or population.
- The 14 September lag source (per-kill node spawning) is gone from every
  build; the dense fragment benchmark holds p95 5.0-6.0 ms.

This is not a claim of performance success from headless alone: the
rendered runs above are scripted, on an integrated GPU, and a hand
playtest is still owed.

## 4. Presets

`data/ascension/presets_v4.json`: for each discipline `<Name> early` (the
authored route's first four locals), `<Name> developed` (through the Q, its
first mutation and the fork) and `<Name> pure` (the authored build plus the
keystone that touches it, Q + V + keystone equipped); `Hybrid: …` for the
six authored hybrids and the three review hybrids; `Ascendant: Three-Core
avalanche` with the Reaction Q and second Revelation equipped. Load any of
them from the overlay's Run tab (Load route). Test saves and probe reports
never touch the player's profile: the suites build their own ledger on a
fresh attempt and the probe writes only to the path it is given.

## 5. Known limitations and interpretations

Every interpretation is dated in the plan's "Decisions and interpretations"
list; the ones a designer should read first:

- Momentum reuses the Manifestation pool (0-1 read as 0-100) with the
  pool's own fill and bleed; dashes add 15; No Brakes' overflow is engine
  owned. Lunge counts as a dash, so Burnout leaves 15 after a Lunge.
- Bastion prevention and armor changes are per-source incoming-damage
  multipliers; Thorns reads the prevented share. Last Hit's intercepted
  blow grants no Force.
- Weak Points are runner statuses with an engine-owned 3 s expiry; the hit
  that exposes never consumes. Return Shot reverses the last leg only.
- Deadshot and JUDGEMENT slow world time through Engine.time_scale with a
  real-time budget; automatic casts pick the densest line with no pause.
- Blasts are queued impacts tagged with a blast id; Mines trigger within
  22 px; "occupied cell" is an 80 px cell; Rolling Thunder's lanes are six
  horizontal lanes across the camera rect.
- Sigil pulses resolve inside the engine so Chain Pulse and Choir know the
  distinct-hit count; Echoes release as the runner's Core-strike geometry.
- Forced movement is stepped through the runner's move primitive; a
  trailing body only crosses one that has stopped; "stagger" is a stun (a
  boss converts L of resisted travel into 0.5 s). Repulse shoves the cone
  away from the player and the Ring away from its centre.
- Slingshot's arc is a mirror across the Well plus an R dash toward aim.

Balance: every coefficient is V4's; nothing has been tuned from play.

## 6. Migration notes

`2026-09-16-v4-migration-boundary.md`: definitions, topology, runtime
state, behaviour and presentation are separated today; ids and save keys
are stable; engines expose an `EFFECTS` table (node id to effect
identifier). The JSON stays the only authoritative source. A one-discipline
pilot (Momentum) is designed there and deliberately not built.

## 7. How to rerun everything

`docs/design/v4_build_test_checklist.md`: the headless regression (about
12 minutes plus the preset suite), the build probe commands headless and
rendered, and the hand playtest pass with what to look at. Never run the
engine while a playtest is in progress on the same machine.
