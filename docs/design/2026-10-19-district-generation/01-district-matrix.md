# District architecture and generation matrix

2026-10-19. All new architectural treatments below are proposals. Exact IDs, terrain pairs and special encounter roles are current code. A/B means a seeded alternative within a segment, not two sequential districts.

## Common visual foundation

Keep broad limestone/plaster, slate/clay and muted iron surfaces readable at the native camera. Express local identity through massing, roof profile, opening rhythm and a few purposeful assemblies before adding small surface detail. The anchor's useful features are substantial masonry returns, connected roof masses, deep entries, grounded planting and a consistent sun direction. Avoid procedural lines across every wall, roof and pavement tile.

Use the existing northeast light/southwest cast-shadow convention in `WorldDepthStyle`. Lamps belong on a wall, post or working assembly; keep their warm light local. A later district does not automatically mean night, heavy fog, neon coverage or beige desert ground. Trees and grass remain temperate; wetter districts change moisture and planting placement, rather than becoming another climate.

Every family needs all four entrance directions and L/recessed footprints. Preserve actual entry notches and indoor coverage. A facade variant may make an existing workshop read as an office or foundry annex; a different physical building needs a separately validated footprint/interior template.

## Asset production contract

For each commissioned family provide a native-camera exterior, entered cutaway and all-direction entry examples, rather than only a beauty illustration. A family packet must include roof mass/material, wall/entry modules, one landmark assembly, three small purpose-specific prop assemblies, neutral ground context and a scale reference to the existing 64 px cell/player. Reuse common lamps, vegetation and material textures where their construction fits.

Record support/mount/feet anchors, physical or purely visual status, maximum visible extent, cutaway attachment, shadow height/direction and whether the object can cross a chunk seam. Objective-coloured lights, collectible-looking objects and door-like painted openings need explicit review so decoration cannot masquerade as interaction. First S3 asset quantities are deliberately smaller; see the delivery plan.

### S3 plate-03 inspection and translation limits

The image provides a useful contrast to S2: a low deep loggia with thick arched masonry and an attached compact watch mass; taller official windows, hip/gable roof hierarchy and grouped iron/copper security fittings distinguish the neighbouring guard office. The pilot combines these into one family, rather than commissioning two independent building systems. Slate, pale stone and muted green cloth are supporting material cues. The plate's numerous tile courses, ornate fittings and greenery are not a requirement to increase runtime micro-detail.

The picture also places banners, grilles, sandbags, gates and tall piers near apparent openings. Runtime placement must follow real traversable cells and actor visibility: never copy a banner across the player's true doorway or imply an open walkable arcade behind a blocked wall. Non-entry arch bays are visibly closed/grilled inspection bays; the actual entrance is the passable bay. The watch mass is an architectural cap on existing support, not a new upper floor, stair destination or gate mechanic.
