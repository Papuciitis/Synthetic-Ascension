# First city opening PeeP and collectible lore proposal

Reviewed writing, 16 October 2026. Four illustrated opening stills establish a working city, an impossible result and the cost of protecting it. A short PeeP encounter makes terminal loss and continuation understandable. Eight optional source documents give the Library human voices beyond its existing milestone summaries.

The user approved this writing direction and implementation on 16 October 2026, with the opening first, followed by PeeP and the eight documents. Existing lines are identified where reused. The unfinished painted concept is the selected style base for every shot. Implementation and verification status are tracked in the dated audits; this record preserves the reviewed writing and compositions. [The representative synthesis scene](2026-10-16-first-city-representative-scene.md) includes the style reference and complete image prompts.

## Story direction and boundaries

The opening should make the player care about the work before being asked to protect it. The researcher makes the breakthrough; Bren understands its lattice behaviour and commits to preserving and spreading it. The city contains useful services and ordinary people as well as coercive authority. Its response becomes more dangerous through the chapter.

The recommended treatment puts four brief stills between existing player actions. A longer narrated history would establish more background but delay the invention. Putting every detail in optional papers would keep the opening faster but leave its human stakes too easy to miss. The four-still treatment gives the main story a clear spine and leaves interpretation to the Library.

Preserve these foundations:

- The protagonist begins as a mortal researcher. The surviving account records stable manufactured synthesis without a patron or awakened bloodline. Recognitional wards cannot classify it.
- Bren is the Lattice Specialist and first Follower, not the inventor. Bren carries the original sequences through the records conduit and does not remember other attempts.
- Calibration, the construct and the officer remain playable. The officer orders surrender and seizure. Deliberate resistance precedes the officer's death and the authorisation of lethal response. Current play has no completed surrender branch; this treatment adds none.
- Ordinary reconstruction keeps the attempt. PeeP appears at terminal defeat, after ordinary reconstruction can no longer continue. Followers do not fund PeeP's intervention.
- Continuing through PeeP begins a fresh attempt. It does not establish that the entire city literally rewinds. The creator, mechanism and appearance of PeeP remain open.
- Segment 10 is the escape from Area I. The faithful's later use of the name Syn'Tek can remain without declaring achieved godhood. Beasts and magical or celestial opposition beyond the city belong to the longer ascent.

The researcher and Bren can remain anonymous silhouettes in this pass. Registry seals, ward bands, the records conduit and later tree marks provide continuity without a crowded symbol vocabulary.

Use the unfinished painted version as the base for every shot: broad visible strokes, sketch edges, simplified forms, grouped shadows, selective detail and quiet areas of exposed underpainting. ## Optional first city source documents

These eight documents supplement the existing 19 concise Chronicle records. They do not replace their IDs, discovery conditions or summaries. The excerpt is what an optional field inspection displays. Choosing to keep a copy discovers its fuller Chronicles text. New titles, anonymous authors, details of daily work and placements are all proposals.

Field inspections occur at safe edges or in the hub, without a timer, combat interruption or required reward. An alert such as “Chronicle copied — read in Library” is enough; the full text waits in Chronicles. The player may close the excerpt without collecting it. No mandatory line of the main story depends on any of these papers.

**Proposed ID:** `city_service_lamp`. **Source:** Registry ward-maintenance worker's handover slip. **Discovery:** inspect the clipped slip beside the admission desk after “admitted”. **Catch-up:** a duplicate on the optional papers tray at the first accessible hub after completing S1.

>

## Discovery and persistence proposal

Physical inspections and the hub papers tray are new functionality. The existing Library already has Chronicles, sealed hints, unread state, search, pagination and bookmarks, backed by persistent Grimoire discoveries. Reuse that foundation rather than creating a parallel lore inventory or mixing these documents into Augments and Recipes.

The proposed tray sits beside the Quiet Alcove's existing Browse entry. It is a recovery source for copies, not a new currency shop. Offer a title and the same field excerpt; selecting “Keep a copy” discovers the Chronicle. Dismissed papers remain available. Completed discoveries have no second pickup reward. Nobody needs to lose an attempt to visit the tray or receive a page.

In field locations, use a modest paper silhouette or Registry seal and an optional interaction prompt when nearby. At most one alert follows collection. Do not tint the whole combat route, pause a live horde or make the collectible look like required exit guidance. If the field area cannot safely host inspection, use the hub source for that paper in the first implementation.

Availability and discovery must be separate. A milestone can make a paper available; only the player's optional inspection and collection marks it discovered. Store each discovery and its read/bookmark state in the existing profile layer before leaving the interaction. Ordinary reconstruction, terminal pending, menu/quit and accepting PeeP must preserve it.

For existing saves, use durable completed milestones, saved best progress or Area I completion to make earlier papers available on the tray. Interpret best completed segment as completion evidence: completed S3 proves S3 was reached and cleared, not that the player has already entered S4. A current Doctrine commitment can prove its own stage was completed, not every later segment. Do not infer that the player inspected an old note or silently mark all old pages read. If durable evidence is insufficient, offer the paper when the player next reaches its ordinary milestone, through successful play as well as after any continuation.

These eight pages have no trigger tied to death, reconstruction count, repeated failures, Follower farming, a random dialogue roll or a particular S4/S6 route variant. Optional clues in sealed entries should describe the source without giving away the prose: for example, “A copy of the night's orders circulates through the service yards.” Keep all 19 current records and their discovery history intact.
