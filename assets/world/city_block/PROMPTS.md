# Built-in ImageGen prompts

All six calls generated new assets without referenced images. The agreed
study 08 informed these production briefs; the concept was not sliced into
runtime artwork. Transparent background was true for arcade, house and plant,
false for the three material fields.

## civic_arcade.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Production GAME SPRITE, not scene concept. Create ONE isolated modular civic arcade facade strip on TRANSPARENT background. Orthographic north-up top-down 2D RPG, steep overhead camera: only shallow SOUTH-facing vertical facade and small top ledge visible, NO side faces, NO isometric vanishing, NO ground. Wide horizontal 4:1 strip with THREE rounded Roman arches, cool pale grey/chalk limestone piers, dark desaturated blue-grey shadowed recesses; centre bay MUST remain a fully open passage with TRANSPARENT opening all the way to bottom (no door, no stairs, no threshold painted across it); left/right bays can be dark recesses with very small iron rail. Mediterranean civic building reused over medieval centuries, later one coarse aged copper service riser on far right pier, modest sage hanging cloth on outer left pier; no heraldry/logos/text/skulls. Bright temperate daylight lit upper right. HAND PAINTED broad muted shapes, restrained selective wear, low-contrast stone joints only 3-4 stone blocks per pier, simple arch voussoirs no black outlines per stone. Strong architectural mass and clean silhouette, no glow, no gold ornament, no ivy forest, no objects projecting across centre doorway. Asset fills image width, neutral no scene background or cast shadow outside object. Entire object and alpha padding visible. Must read as a shallow facade at compact gameplay size; the architecture's internal lines softer than character outlines.

## slate.png

Use case stylized-concept. Production seamless material texture tile for a 2D north-up overhead medieval Mediterranean science-fantasy game. Flat orthographic surface viewed DIRECTLY from above: dark blue-grey SLATE roofing. Broad grouped soft slate tile shapes in horizontal staggered rows, roughly eight tiles across and eight rows tall. Quiet hand-painted material, small low-contrast seams WITHOUT BLACK OUTLINES, no hard highlights, no moss speckle, no cracks covering every tile. Three grouped value families, diffuse neutral daylight, uniform flat lighting (game shader adds roof slopes). Full square canvas completely filled with repeatable slate texture, edges tile seamlessly, no borders, no ridge/eaves/chimney, no perspective, no text. At gameplay scale it should resolve as a calm blue grey surface with selective subtle slate shapes, not a high frequency checkerboard. Not photorealistic, not pixel art, painterly restrained game material.

## clay.png

Use case stylized-concept. Seamless square flat overhead GAME ROOF MATERIAL: muted weathered terracotta clay pantiles for temperate medieval Mediterranean European city. Directly orthographic top view no perspective, full image surface. About 12 softly grouped tiles across, 12 horizontal staggered rows. ## pressure_plant.png

Use case stylized-concept. One isolated production 2D top-down RPG game sprite on TRANSPARENT background: wall-mounted old municipal water pressure plant, narrow vertical unit. One squat cylindrical oxidized teal-green copper reservoir with dark iron bands, rounded top visible from steep overhead, two chunky elbows/valves, coarse pipes running parallel on right, one small analog brass gauge and charcoal junction box. Mediterranean medieval building retrofitted with early industrial cybernetic utilities. Painterly muted broad shapes, clear simple silhouette, sparse clean detail, diffuse bright daylight from upper right. Orthographic north-up game view steep overhead (see top surfaces and short south faces), NOT isometric, no background/ground/wall, no platform, no scene shadows, no labels/text, no gold ornament, no neon. Object fully visible with alpha padding. At game size only about 45x110 pixels so strong massing, no micro etching, no outlines on every joint.
